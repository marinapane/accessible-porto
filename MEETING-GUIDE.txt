ACCESSIBLE PORTO — MEETING PROTOTYPE

OPENING THE DEMO
Double-click index.html. It works offline in a modern web browser.
No install, sign-in, spreadsheet connection, or internet is required.
Bring the full project folder on your laptop or a USB drive so the sample
database downloads work. Keep a printed copy as a backup.
Use “Print meeting copy” on the page to print or save a PDF.

SUGGESTED FIVE-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH
1. Explain that the name is provisional and all eight listings are fictional.
2. Search for “taxi” and open the result’s details.
3. Clear filters, then select “Step-free access” and “Hearing support”.
4. Clear the filters and reopen Central Porto Taxi to compare Yes, No,
   and Unknown access information.
5. Use the Meeting guide at the bottom to discuss the decisions below.

DECISIONS FOR THE GROUP
- Audience: residents, visitors, carers, or everyone?
- Coverage: Porto city initially, or Greater Porto?
- Priorities: which three categories should be populated first?
- Languages: Portuguese and English; who will review translations?
- Access: which specific features should be recorded, and how?
- Trust: who checks provider claims and community reports?
- Ownership: who will manage the project accounts and maintain the site?
- Contributions: who moderates suggested listings and corrections?
- Pilot: who can contribute and check the first 20–30 real listings?

PROPOSED FIRST SPREADSHEET
Listings tab: stable ID, name, category ID, description in Portuguese and
English, area, address, public contact details, website, languages, hours,
cost or eligibility, access feature values (Yes / No / Unknown), access
notes, source, date checked, and publication status.
Categories tab: stable ID and Portuguese/English category names.
Parishes tab: the seven official parishes used in the Area filter.
Keep private submissions and contributor contacts in a separate restricted
file. Only approved public fields should be published to the website.
Defer public reviews until there is a moderation process.

QUESTIONS FOR LUIS
- May we see a blank copy of your spreadsheet structure?
- How do approved spreadsheet updates reach the website?
- What happens if the spreadsheet is unavailable?
- Is any code available to reuse, and under what licence?
- Who maintains the site, and how are corrections handled?

WHAT WORKS NOW
Keyword search, category and area filters, combined access filters,
listing detail dialogs, keyboard operation, responsive layout, and printing.
There are no third-party fonts, scripts, analytics, or network dependencies.

WHAT IS STILL PROPOSED
Real listings, Google Sheets publishing, suggestion forms, translations,
hosting, a final project name, community validation, and a full accessibility
review with disabled participants. This prototype is not a WCAG conformance
certification and must not be presented as a verified service directory.

EXAMPLE DATABASE
Open outputs/porto-demo/porto-example-database.xlsx in Excel or import it into
Google Sheets. It contains Listings, Categories, Parishes, Reviews, and Editing guide.
The workbook and CSVs match the examples on the site. All entries are
fictional. Editing these files does not update the website automatically.

PHONE LAYOUT
The page adapts to narrow screens with stacked search controls, one-column
listing cards, larger touch targets, wrapping navigation, and full-width
action buttons. Transfer the files to a device to try them locally. A shared
web address will require hosting, which is not configured in this prototype.
